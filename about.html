A Little Bit About Me

I was adopted from Guatemala and have lived in Council Bluffs, Iowa all my life. Family, community, and the people around me have shaped the way I think about engineering and the kind of person I want to be.

Why Engineering

When I chose Computer Engineering it was mainly because I was strong in math and science, and my family encouraged me to give engineering a real shot. I chose CPRE because I had taken a HTML class at my highschool and found it interesting.

My real obsesion with egineering itself came from reading The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind. Seeing how innovation could improve people's lives made engineering feel less like just a major and more like a way to solve meaningful problems.

Community And Perspective

I have always cared about adding to my community, especially through outreach opportunities near where I grew up. Those experiences taught me how important it is to make people feel welcome, respected, and seen.

Throughout my coursework, I have learned that engineering is not only about finding the right technical answer. It is also about understanding the ethical implications, like who is affected by a decision, and learning from people who see the situation differently.

How I Work

A good and respectible engineer colaboates with patience, and accepts critisism. The engineer cares more about the best result than about being right.

Throughout my courses at Iowa State, I have gotten chances to sharpen my collaboration skills. To make a team project best flow I have found that getting everyones scheduals down helps a lot. Also agreeing on where the code will be shared. I have learned that I do not have all the answers, or maybe someone else answer is just more effcient.

Outside The Classroom

Since I started college I have really fallen in love with the gym and taking nature walks. I also love going bowling or playing pool when I get the chance.

College has had so many challenges and learning curves but I would not undo a single one. I have grown so much as a problem solver, teammate, and person. I am so excited to see where this career will take me.
