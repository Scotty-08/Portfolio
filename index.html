Scott Gray

I was adopted from Guatemala and grew up in Council Bluffs, Iowa. Engineering stopped being abstract for me when my dad fell from a zipline and spent months in rehabilitation.

At Madonna Rehabilitation Hospital I watched robotics-assisted therapy help him relearn to move. That is the work I want to do: hardware and software that give people some of their life back.

Now I'm a junior at Iowa State. I build web apps, iOS apps and Android apps, and I'm looking for internships.
