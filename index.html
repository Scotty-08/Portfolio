          About me

          I was adopted from Guatemala and I've lived in Council Bluffs, Iowa my whole life. Now I'm studying computer engineering at Iowa State.

          I got into computer engineering because I was good at math and science, and an HTML class got me interested in coding. What made the decision was learning how computer engineers work with robotics.

          When I was young, my dad fell from a zipline, and the fall left his left side paralyzed. His therapist guided him through recovery, and the robotics at Madonna Rehabilitation Hospital helped too. That technology is the main reason I chose computer engineering.

          I want to build on the technology we have today so it can help people like my dad heal, or make their everyday lives easier.
